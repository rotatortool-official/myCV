Valheim Music Plus

2026

Published music mod for the survival game Valheim, released on Thunderstore under the handle Emberskald. It replaces the game's soundtrack with an original score that follows the player, switching between nine biomes, the home base and boss encounters, with shuffle queues, fade transitions and resumption of the track a zone was left on. Built on the BepInEx modding framework; the score was composed with Suno and players can drop their own audio into the same folders.
